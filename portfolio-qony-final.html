FEATURED
Form Bukti Pembayaran
A payment proof submission form for Users — built on Google Apps Script + Sheets at zero infrastructure cost.
The Problems
  • Users frequently made input errors in standard Google Forms — wrong amounts, empty fields — leaving the treasurer to manually reconcile
  • Google Forms couldn't pull per-student arrears from the database to auto-generate checkboxes
  • No IT budget: the school needed a real solution at Rp0
  • Most users have low digital literacy — login flows caused immediate drop-off
What Was Built

Login removed entirely. Users type their child's name — autocomplete surfaces matches — then check off outstanding payments from an auto-generated list pulled live from the school's spreadsheet. Multiple children can be handled in one submission. Uploaded payment photos enter a manual verification queue so the treasurer can cross-check against bank mutations before marking anything as paid. The backend is restricted to expose only Name and NIS during search, keeping the source spreadsheet private.

Results
  • Zero input errors — all payment details are selected, not typed
  • Usable by users with minimal tech experience
  • Runs on Google's free infrastructure — total cost: Rp0

FEATURED
Kartu Pelajar Generator
A self-service student ID card generator — students verify their identity and receive their card by email in under 2 minutes.
The Problems
  • Admin had to process every card request manually — high demand, limited time
  • The previous Google Form setup required admin to maintain dropdown lists by hand to avoid typos
  • Students consistently didn't know their own NIS/NISN, causing constant interruptions to admin
  • With autocrat, files piled up in Drive until storage filled
What Was Built

Students verify with three pieces of information they actually know: full name, date of birth, and mother's name. If those match the database, the system pulls NIS, NISN, and address automatically. The card is generated in-memory (no Drive storage used) and sent to the student's email immediately. No account creation needed. The verification triple acts as a privacy gatekeeper, preventing students from accessing each other's records.

Results
  • Students generate their own card in under 2 minutes, any time, without admin involvement
  • Zero Drive storage consumed — no file accumulation
  • Runs on Google's free infrastructure — total cost: Rp0
